**Description**

```
FITgap closes the gap between your training account and your iPhone: it downloads the original FIT files of your activities from Wahoo and Zwift and hands them to the app of your choice, such as HealthFit, using the native iOS share sheet.

ORIGINAL FILES, UNCHANGED
Every FIT file is stored byte for byte exactly as the provider delivers it – no conversion, no re-encoding. A checksum ensures that exactly this file is shared.

HOW IT WORKS
• Connect Wahoo or Zwift under "Accounts"
• Refresh – all available activities and FIT files are downloaded, existing ones are skipped
• Tap Share and choose the destination in the share sheet

NEW IN VERSION 0.2.0
• Activity details: distance, duration, speed, calories, heart rate, cadence, power, elevation gain and sport – read directly from the FIT file
• Share several activities at once: select them or "Select All" and hand them over together
• All FIT files are also available in the Files app under "On My iPhone › FITgap", with readable names based on date, time and source
• Optimized for iPhone Duo and wide displays: list on the left, details on the right

CLEAR OVERVIEW
• Distance, elevation gain and average speed right in the list
• Search across all activities
• "Last shared" marker with date and time
• Large archives with hundreds of activities are downloaded reliably and can resume after interruptions

PRIVATE AND LOCAL
• No server of its own, no tracking, no ads
• Sign-in tokens are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain
• Your FIT files stay on your device until you share them yourself

TRY IT WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
With "Try Demo" you can explore FITgap with synthetic sample rides – no sign-in required.

Supports Light and Dark Mode, English and German, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver. FITgap is open source (MIT License).

Notes: Through its API, Wahoo only provides its own workouts, not workouts imported from third parties. The Zwift connection does not use an official public API and may be affected by changes on Zwift's side. FITgap is an independent app and is not affiliated with Wahoo Fitness, Zwift or HealthFit.
```

## App Review Information (English only)

**Sign-In Information:** Sign-in required: **off**. All features can be reviewed in the built-in demo mode without an account.

**Notes**

```
Thank you for reviewing FITgap 0.2.0.

WHAT THE APP DOES
FITgap downloads the original FIT activity files from the user's own Wahoo or Zwift account and stores them locally on the device. The user can then hand these files to another app (for example HealthFit) using the standard iOS share sheet (UIActivityViewController). The files are never modified. FITgap has no backend server, no analytics, no ads and no in-app purchases. It does not use HealthKit.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT (DEMO MODE)
No account or sign-in is needed to review the app:
1. Launch the app. On the main screen, tap "Try Demo" (shown above the activity list while no account is connected; also available under "Accounts").
2. Three fully synthetic sample rides appear. The banner "Demo – Sample Data" stays visible at the bottom of the screen while demo mode is active.
3. Tap an activity to open its details (distance, duration, speed, heart rate, elevation, sport).
4. Tap the share button on an activity row to open the iOS share sheet with a real local file named "FITgap-Demo-….fit".
5. Multi-select (new in 0.2.0): tap "Select" in the navigation bar, pick several activities or tap "Select All", then tap "Share" to hand all selected files over in one share sheet.
6. "Accounts" contains "Reset Demo" and "End Demo", plus the link to the privacy policy.
Demo mode makes no network requests and does not touch any real account data. The demo FIT files were generated synthetically with Garmin's official FIT SDK and contain no personal data or GPS coordinates. Demo files are not copied to the Files app.

NEW IN 0.2.0
- Activity details view, read only from the summary values in the local FIT file.
- Sharing multiple activities in one share sheet.
- Files app integration: downloaded real FIT files are additionally copied to the app's Documents folder (UIFileSharingEnabled and LSSupportsOpeningDocumentsInPlace are enabled), so they appear in the Files app under "On My iPhone › FITgap". The user can copy or delete them there; nothing outside the app's own container is accessed.
- Two-column layout (NavigationSplitView) on wide displays such as an unfolded iPhone Duo; landscape is enabled only on such wide displays, regular iPhones stay in portrait.

ACCOUNT CONNECTIONS (OPTIONAL, USER'S OWN ACCOUNTS ONLY)
- Wahoo: standard OAuth 2.0 with PKCE (S256) through ASWebAuthenticationSession, using FITgap's registered Wahoo API application. Requested scopes are read-only: user_read workouts_read. No client secret is embedded in the app.
- Zwift: the user signs in with their own Zwift credentials. The password is used only once to obtain a session token directly from Zwift and is not stored. Please note that Zwift does not offer an official public API for this; FITgap only reads the user's own activity list and FIT files.
- Tokens are stored only in the iOS Keychain (WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly, not synced to iCloud). "Disconnect" removes the local session.
FITgap is an independent app and is not affiliated with Wahoo Fitness, Zwift or HealthFit. These names are used only to describe compatibility.

BACKGROUND BEHAVIOR
A running download may continue for the short extra time iOS grants after the app moves to the background (beginBackgroundTask). The app does not use any background modes and does not run permanently in the background.

PRIVACY AND ENCRYPTION
No user data is collected by the developer; all data stays on the device. Privacy policy: https://florianwildt.github.io/fitgap-info/
The app uses only standard HTTPS provided by iOS (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).

The full source code is publicly available under the MIT License: https://github.com/florianwildt/fitgap

If you have any questions, please contact us through App Store Connect. Thank you!
```
